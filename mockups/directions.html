What changes in every direction

  1. One place to add things. The “Tell Higgins” bar and the big mic button in the middle of the tab bar. It files instantly — no forms — and shows a receipt you can Undo or Move.
  2. Home is a briefing. Today’s handful of things first, then colored tiles to jump into a list. No gauges up top.
  3. Light by default. Dark mode becomes an option, not automatic.
  4. Details are optional. Tap any item to add notes, cost, photos or history later.
